Privacy Policy

Worldoku — Last updated: May 2025

This policy explains what information Worldoku ("we", "our", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect only what is necessary to run the game and improve it.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (optional)

You can play Worldoku as a guest without creating an account. If you choose to sign up, we collect your email address and the username you pick. We use these to identify your account and let you track your stats across devices.

Game results

When you complete a puzzle, we record your score, number of wrong guesses, hints used, time taken, difficulty level, and the answers you submitted. This data is linked to your account (if signed in) and is used to maintain leaderboards, award badges, and show answer-distribution statistics to all players.

Usage events

We log basic in-app events (for example: "game started", "account created") to understand how the app is being used. These events are stored on our own servers. They are never sold or shared with third-party analytics providers. Events from guest sessions are not linked to any personal identity.

Device information

We record which platform (iOS, Android, or web) a game was played on. We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or precise location.

2. Advertising

Worldoku shows ads between puzzles using Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC). On iOS, we ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before allowing AdMob to use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • If you allow tracking: AdMob may use the IDFA to show personalised ads and measure their performance.
  • If you decline: AdMob will only show non-personalised ads. Your IDFA is not shared with advertisers.

You can change your tracking preference at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and sync your game progress.
  • To display leaderboards, badges, and answer statistics.
  • To understand how players interact with the game and improve it.
  • To serve ads (see Section 2).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to operating and improving Worldoku.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored securely on servers provided by Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA). Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

In-progress puzzle state is also cached locally on your device using iOS/Android storage. This data never leaves your device and is cleared when you sign out or delete your account.

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Aggregate, non-identifiable game statistics may be retained indefinitely.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated personal data directly within the app: go to Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent — your email, username, game history, and all related records are removed from our servers. This cannot be undone.

If you have any other data requests (access, correction, portability), please contact us using the address in Section 8.

6. Children

Worldoku is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
